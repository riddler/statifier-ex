# Research: What the Document-to-Machine compiler pass must consume and produce

**Date**: 2026-08-09T07:19:08-0600
**Git Commit**: 4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828
**Branch**: st-wju.1-compile-document-machine
**Bead**: st-wju.1 (Compiles the Document to an interned Machine)

## Research Question

Document the codebase as it exists today in everything the st-wju.1 compiler
pass must consume and produce: the full `Statifier.Document` struct tree and
what each node carries; how source locations are attached; what lowering and
the validator already compute and what they deliberately do not; the current
predicator dependency and the exact API surface for compiling an expression
with a position side table; how foo/fooexpr attribute pairs appear on the
Document today; whether anything in `lib/` already consumes predicator; and
what ADR-0005, ADR-0012, ADR-0014 and the design docs commit to for the
Machine layout, interning, and expression storage. Also survey the v1 engine
at `../statifier` for how it laid out its compiled form, as a reference point
rather than a template.

## Summary

The input side is settled and complete. `Statifier.Document` is a purely
syntactic tree: nine structs, no derived fields, no indexes, no parent
pointers, no depth, no resolved targets, no compiled expressions. Document
order is preserved in every child list, and every node carries a
`Statifier.Parser.Location` six-field span plus an `attribute_locations` map
of per-attribute **value** spans that exists only for attributes the author
actually wrote. Lowering applies the spec's own attribute defaults and splits
`event`/`target`/`initial` on whitespace; the validator confirms 11 structural
and semantic invariants and then hands the *same* document back unmodified. It
computes parent, ancestor and descendant relations only inside an ephemeral
`Statifier.Validator.Context` that its own moduledoc forbids the compiler from
reusing. Everything the bead names as compiler work is genuinely unclaimed.

The output side is specified by ADR-0005 (flat document-order array, parent
pointers, contiguous descendant ranges, integer/range comparisons,
`MapSet`s of integers, both index directions on the Machine), ADR-0012 item 3
(locations retained on states, transitions and executable content, plus
compiler-assigned document-order indexes for transitions and content), and
ADR-0014 (the expression sum type and its span table). `docs/observability.md`
turns those into an explicit implementation checklist.

The single live tension is the expression storage shape, and it moved on the
day of this research. The docs and the `Statifier.Document` moduledoc all state
the compiled arm as `{:compiled, %Predicator.Compiled{}, source}`, which is
predicator's upstream envelope from predicator ADR-0009 and ships in
**predicator 4.0**. This repo's pin is `~> 3.8` and `mix.lock` holds 3.8.0,
which has no `Predicator.Compiled` module and no `compile_with_spans/1`; at
that version `Predicator.compile_with_positions/1` returns a **three-element**
tuple `{:ok, instructions, positions}` and fails with a bare formatted
**string**, not a struct with a `:position` field.

**predicator 4.0.0 was published to hex on 2026-08-09**, the date of this
research (`mix hex.info predicator`: `4.0.0 (2026-08-09)`, `Locked version:
3.8.0`). That resolves ADR-0014's own conditional in favour of the span branch
- the ADR says whoever wires cond "checks whether 4.0 shipped and whether
px-35i.5 settled, then follows items 1-2", and both are now yes. It does not
by itself move this repo: the pin bump is bead **st-2pj**, still open at P3,
whose acceptance criteria include sweeping the corpus converters from `=` to
`==` before the bump. So the choice for st-wju.1 is between depending on
st-2pj and taking ADR-0014 item 1's explicitly pre-authorized point-position
stopgap. Recorded under Open Questions; both branches are now live options
rather than one being impossible.

Nothing under `lib/` calls predicator today. The only real call site in the
repo is the corpus tool exercised by `test/corpus/check_exprs_test.exs`.

## Detailed Findings

### 1. The `Statifier.Document` struct tree

Nine modules under `lib/statifier/document/` plus the root. Field lists as
they stand:

| Struct | Enforced | Fields |
|---|---|---|
| `Statifier.Document` | `:location` | `name`, `version`, `xmlns`, `namespace`, `datamodel`, `binding` (`:early`), `initial: [String.t()]`, `states: [State.t()]`, `attribute_locations` |
| `Statifier.Document.State` | `:kind`, `:location` | `id: String.t() \| nil`, `initial: [String.t()]`, `initial_element: Initial.t() \| nil`, `states: [t()]`, `transitions: [Transition.t()]`, `onentry: [Block.t()]`, `onexit: [Block.t()]`, `history_type: :shallow \| :deep \| nil`, `donedata: Donedata.t() \| nil`, `attribute_locations` |
| `Statifier.Document.Transition` | `:location` | `cond: String.t() \| nil`, `event: [String.t()]`, `target: [String.t()]`, `type: :internal \| :external` (default `:external`), `content: [content_node()]`, `attribute_locations` |
| `Statifier.Document.Initial` | `:location` | `transitions: [Transition.t()]` |
| `Statifier.Document.Block` | `:location` | `content: [content_node()]` |
| `Statifier.Document.Donedata` | `:location` | `content: Content.t() \| nil` |
| `Statifier.Document.Content` | `:location` | `expr: String.t() \| nil`, `text: String.t() \| nil`, `attribute_locations` |
| `Statifier.Document.Log` | `:location` | `label: String.t() \| nil`, `expr: String.t() \| nil`, `attribute_locations` |
| `Statifier.Document.Raise` | `:event`, `:location` | `attribute_locations` |

Key type facts:

- `@type state_kind :: :state | :parallel | :final | :history`
  ([`lib/statifier/document.ex:109`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document.ex#L109)). `:scxml` is not a member, and neither are
  the compiler-only widenings `:atomic` / `:compound`. The moduledoc states
  the compiler's own `kind` field is unconstrained by that layer
  ([`lib/statifier/document/state.ex:33-34`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document/state.ex#L33-L34)).
- `@type content_node :: Raise.t() | Log.t()` ([`lib/statifier/document.ex:101`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document.ex#L101))
  is the whole Phase 1 executable-content set.
- `<scxml>` is deliberately its own struct rather than a `State` with
  `kind: :scxml`. The moduledoc argues the point and ends by saying st-wju.1
  "synthesizes its index-0 root state from these fields directly rather than
  copying a `%State{}` that never existed at this layer"
  ([`lib/statifier/document.ex:58-87`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document.ex#L58-L87)).
- `<initial>` is a slot (`State.initial_element`), never a kind, because it has
  no `id` ([`lib/statifier/document/initial.ex:1-29`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document/initial.ex#L1-L29)). `Initial.transitions` is
  a list even though the spec requires exactly one, so the wrong shape survives
  long enough for the validator to report it.
- `Transition.event` and `Transition.target` are already whitespace-split
  lists. The moduledoc states the *second* level split of a descriptor into
  dot-separated tokens is st-wju.1's job "once the descriptor is being matched
  against a compiled event name, not stored"
  ([`lib/statifier/document/transition.ex:6-18`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document/transition.ex#L6-L18)).
- Transition content is `[content_node()]` directly, not wrapped in a `Block`,
  because there is no wrapping element to give a block its own location
  ([`lib/statifier/document/transition.ex:34-37`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document/transition.ex#L34-L37)).

No struct carries a `parent`, `depth`, `index`, `document_order`, or a state
lookup map. The moduledoc says so directly: "Interning, indexing, and
expression compilation are the compiler's job, not this layer's - a
`%Statifier.Document{}` tree is walkable and unambiguous, not fast"
([`lib/statifier/document.ex:7-10`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document.ex#L7-L10)).

### 2. Source locations

`Statifier.Parser.Location` is a six-field exclusive-end span: 1-based
`start_line`/`start_column`/`end_line`/`end_column` (columns in Unicode
codepoints) and 0-based `start_offset`/`end_offset`
([`lib/statifier/parser/location.ex:13-37`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/parser/location.ex#L13-L37)). Two helpers exist:
`at_offset/2` ([`lib/statifier/parser/location.ex:46-58`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/parser/location.ex#L46-L58)) and `slice/2`
([`lib/statifier/parser/location.ex:68-72`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/parser/location.ex#L68-L72)), the latter being the primitive the
location-accuracy sweep and the validator's enum checks use.

Locations reach the compiler two ways:

1. **Node location.** Every struct's `location` is copied verbatim from the
   DOM element ([`lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex:59`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex#L59),
   [`lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex:164`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex#L164),
   [`lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex:358`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex#L358)). Nothing strips it.
2. **Attribute value spans.** `attribute_locations` is
   `%{optional(atom()) => Location.t()}` ([`lib/statifier/document.ex:98`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document.ex#L98)),
   keyed by attribute name atom and valued by the span of the text *inside the
   quotes*. Its defining rule: an entry exists only for an attribute the author
   actually wrote ([`lib/statifier/document.ex:31-39`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document.ex#L31-L39)), which makes
   `Map.has_key?(node.attribute_locations, :type)` exactly the "was it written"
   question. `Statifier.Lowering.Attributes.put_location/4`
   ([`lib/statifier/lowering/attributes.ex:76-98`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering/attributes.ex#L76-L98)) also skips the entry when the
   parser handler produced a nil `value_location`, so absence of a key is not
   proof of absence of the attribute in that edge case.

The value span is the base offset ADR-0014's arithmetic adds a
within-expression span to: `value_location.start_offset + span.start` is a
document byte offset, convertible back with `Location.at_offset/2`
([`lib/statifier/parser/location.ex:7-10`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/parser/location.ex#L7-L10)). `cond`'s span lives at
`attribute_locations[:cond]`, not on a field of `Transition`
([`lib/statifier/document/transition.ex:20-24`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document/transition.ex#L20-L24)), and `log`'s `expr` span at
`attribute_locations[:expr]` ([`lib/statifier/document/log.ex:7-10`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document/log.ex#L7-L10)).

`Content.text` deliberately has no span of its own - entity references and
CDATA can split the concatenation across several source runs, so the `Content`
node's own `location` is what a diagnostic points at
([`lib/statifier/document/content.ex:17-21`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document/content.ex#L17-L21)).

### 3. What lowering computes

`Statifier.Lowering.lower/1` takes the generic DOM root element and returns
`{:ok, Document.t()} | {:error, [Error.t()]}`
([`lib/statifier/lowering.ex:69-86`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering.ex#L69-L86)). It resolves the root's namespace, checks
the SCXML vocabulary, and dispatches on local element name through a static map
([`lib/statifier/lowering.ex:53-67`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering.ex#L53-L67)) into one builder per element in
`Statifier.Lowering.Builders`. `walk_children/2`
([`lib/statifier/lowering.ex:99-107`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering.ex#L99-L107)) folds the unfiltered child list, dropping
whitespace-only text and erroring on non-whitespace text;
`Builders.place/3` ([`lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex:393-450`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex#L393-L450)) folds each
walked child into the parent's correct slot or reports
`{:misplaced_element, name, parent_name}`.

What it establishes for the compiler:

- **Document order is preserved everywhere.** Children are prepended during the
  fold and reversed once in `reverse_lists/1`
  ([`lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex:456-482`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex#L456-L482)), covering `states`,
  `transitions`, `onentry`, `onexit`, `Initial.transitions`, `Block.content`,
  and `Transition.content`. `walk_children/2` reverses its own accumulator
  ([`lib/statifier/lowering.ex:106`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering.ex#L106)). Source order is list order.
- **Whitespace splitting is done once.** `Attributes.list/2`
  ([`lib/statifier/lowering/attributes.ex:42-46`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering/attributes.ex#L42-L46)) turns `event`, `target`, and
  `initial` into `[String.t()]`; both an absent attribute and `""` become `[]`,
  told apart only by `attribute_locations`.
- **Spec defaults are applied, not left to consumers.** `Attributes.atom/4`
  ([`lib/statifier/lowering/attributes.ex:67-73`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering/attributes.ex#L67-L73)) maps through fixed value
  tables ([`lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex:32-34`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex#L32-L34)) for `binding`
  (`:early`), `history_type` (`:shallow`), and transition `type`
  (`:external`). An out-of-range value silently takes the default here and is
  reported by the validator instead
  ([`lib/statifier/lowering/attributes.ex:52-59`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering/attributes.ex#L52-L59)).
- **`namespace` versus `xmlns`.** `xmlns` is the literal attribute; `namespace`
  is the resolved URI stamped on by `lower/1`. They differ for a
  prefix-declared root ([`lib/statifier/document.ex:46-56`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document.ex#L46-L56)).
- **Errors are collected, never raised.** `finalize/2`
  ([`lib/statifier/lowering.ex:137-141`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering.ex#L137-L141)) sorts errors by
  `location.start_offset`; a non-empty list means `{:error, errors}` and the
  partially-built document is never handed back.

Expression-bearing attributes are stored as raw source and nothing else:
`cond` ([`lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex:167`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex#L167)), `log`'s `expr`
([`lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex:253`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex#L253)), `content`'s `expr`
([`lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex:315`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex#L315)).

### 4. What the validator establishes, and what it deliberately does not

`Statifier.Validator.validate/2` takes the document and its own source binary
and returns `{:ok, Document.t()} | {:error, [Error.t()]}`
([`lib/statifier/validator.ex:61-75`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/validator.ex#L61-L75)). The `source` argument exists so checks
can `Location.slice/2` a raw attribute value back out - lowering has already
defaulted invalid enum values away, so the only way to see what the author
wrote is to re-read the source. **The returned document is the caller's own
input, unmodified** ([`lib/statifier/validator.ex:19-23`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/validator.ex#L19-L23)).

The 11 checks ([`lib/statifier/validator.ex:47-59`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/validator.ex#L47-L59)) and their guarantees:

1. `Checks.Ids` - every non-nil, non-empty state `id` is unique; `id=""` is
   its own error (`{:empty_id}`).
2. `Checks.Targets` - every transition `target` token, across plain,
   `<initial>` and history owners, names a state present in the document. This
   check owns target *existence* exclusively.
3. `Checks.InitialTargets` - every `initial` reference (state attribute,
   `<initial>` element targets, `Document.initial`) resolves and is a legal
   descendant or top-level target; `initial` on a non-compound state is
   flagged.
4. `Checks.InitialElement` - no state carries both an `initial` attribute and
   an `<initial>` element; the element's content model is exactly one
   target-bearing transition with no `event`/`cond`.
5. `Checks.History` - a history state's parent is a compound `<state>` or
   `<parallel>`; its default transition is exactly one target-bearing
   transition with no `event`/`cond`; the target descends from the history's
   parent; a written `type` is literally `"shallow"` or `"deep"`.
6. `Checks.Final` - a `:final` has no state children and no transitions.
7. `Checks.DefaultEntry` - a compound state with no `initial` in either form
   whose first document-order child is `:history` is an error, because spec
   3.3's first-child fallback cannot target a history pseudo-state.
8. `Checks.Donedata` - `donedata` only on `:final`.
9. `Checks.Content` - `<content>` never carries both a non-nil `expr` and
   non-blank text.
10. `Checks.Boilerplate` - the resolved root `namespace` is the SCXML
    namespace and `version` is `"1.0"`.
11. `Checks.Enums` - a written transition `type` is `internal`/`external`, a
    written `binding` is `early`/`late`.

`Checks.DefaultTransition` is a shared sub-check used by 4 and 5, not itself in
the list.

**What it does not do**, all of which is therefore compiler work:

- It does not resolve `initial`. Checks 3 and 4 confirm the referenced id
  exists and descends legally; they never store a resolved state or index, and
  the spec 3.3 first-child default is only checked for its one illegal case,
  never resolved into a chosen default-entry state.
- It does not resolve transition targets. `Transition.target` stays
  `[String.t()]`.
- It never parses, compiles or even syntax-checks an expression. `cond` and
  both `expr` fields stay raw strings. [`lib/statifier/document.ex:14-20`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document.ex#L14-L20) states
  that nothing under `lib/statifier/document/` may reference `Predicator`.
- It assigns no index, no document-order number, no `t_index`/`c_index`.
- It does not widen `kind`. No `:atomic`/`:compound` discriminator is computed
  or stored; `Context.compound?/1`
  ([`lib/statifier/validator/context.ex:76-84`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/validator/context.ex#L76-L84)) computes it ad hoc and is
  explicitly not an Appendix D predicate.
- It never coerces a bad enum value; it only reports it.

Parent, ancestor and descendant relations exist only inside
`Statifier.Validator.Context`, built fresh per call and discarded: `states`
(id to `State.t()`), `ancestors` (id to root-first ancestor id list), `parents`
(struct identity to parent struct, including nameless states), `transitions`
(flat `[{Transition.t(), owner}]`), plus `descendant?/3`
([`lib/statifier/validator/context.ex:69-73`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/validator/context.ex#L69-L73)) as a boolean test rather than a
materialized set. Its moduledoc states outright that it is never to be reused
by the compiler ([`lib/statifier/validator/context.ex:1-8`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/validator/context.ex#L1-L8)). Depth is never
computed at all.

Both layers share an error shape worth copying: `{reason, message, location}`
with a closed tagged-tuple `reason` union declared once in full and a
constructor per reason ([`lib/statifier/lowering/error.ex:21-36`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering/error.ex#L21-L36),
[`lib/statifier/validator/error.ex:33-58`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/validator/error.ex#L33-L58)).

### 5. foo/fooexpr attribute pairs today

**No foo/fooexpr pair exists on any Document struct at present.** The
convention belongs to elements Phase 1 does not build: `<param>` is explicitly
unsupported ([`lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex:269-272`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex#L269-L272)), and `<send>`,
`<assign>`, `<data>`, `<datamodel>`, `<if>`, `<foreach>`, and `<script>` have
no builders. The Phase 1 element set is `scxml, state, parallel, final,
initial, history, transition, onentry, onexit, raise, log, donedata, content`.

What the compiler actually has to compile in Phase 1 is three raw expression
slots, none of which has a static sibling attribute:

| Node | Field | Type today |
|---|---|---|
| `Transition` | `cond` | `String.t() \| nil` |
| `Log` | `expr` | `String.t() \| nil` |
| `Content` | `expr` | `String.t() \| nil` |

`Content` is the closest thing to a pair: `expr` and `text` are both nilable
and both representable at once precisely so `Checks.Content` can report the
spec 5.6 violation ([`lib/statifier/document/content.ex:23-29`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document/content.ex#L23-L29)). Whether the
compiler folds `Content.text`/`Content.expr` into one `expr()` value with the
static arm holding the text is a shape decision the codebase does not settle;
[`docs/architecture.md:80-82`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/docs/architecture.md#L80-L82) ("Every attribute that accepts `foo` / `fooexpr`
pairs stores one `expr` value") is the only guidance, and `Content` is the one
node it plausibly applies to today.

`FeatureDetector` still marks `conditional_transitions: :unsupported`
([`test/support/feature_detector.ex:53`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/test/support/feature_detector.ex#L53)), matched by `~r/\bcond\s*=/`
([`test/support/feature_detector.ex:133`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/test/support/feature_detector.ex#L133)), which is what keeps cond-bearing
corpus documents out of the runtime until Phase 3.

### 6. The predicator dependency and its exact 3.8 API surface

[`mix.exs:41`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/mix.exs#L41) requires `{:predicator, "~> 3.8"}` and [`mix.lock:17`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/mix.lock#L17) pins
**3.8.0**. Note that [`docs/datamodel.md:3-4`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/docs/datamodel.md#L3-L4) and [`docs/adr/0004-predicator-as-the-datamodel.md:16`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/docs/adr/0004-predicator-as-the-datamodel.md#L16)
still say `~> 3.5`; the pin history is st-lqg (3.7 to 3.8, closed) and st-2pj
(3.8 to 4.0, open).

**4.0.0 is on hex as of 2026-08-09.** `mix hex.info predicator` reports
`4.0.0 (2026-08-09)` against `Locked version: 3.8.0`. Everything below the
3.8 subsection describes the pinned version; the 4.0 delta follows it.

At 3.8.0 (`deps/predicator/`):

- **`compile/1`** returns `{:ok, instruction_list} | {:error, binary()}`
  (predicator.ex, `@spec` at line 305).
- **`compile_with_positions/1`** returns a **three-element** tuple:
  `{:ok, Types.instruction_list(), Types.position_table()} | {:error, binary()}`
  (predicator.ex, `@spec` at lines 335-336, body 337-346). The docstring
  example is instructions `[["load", "score"], ["lit", 85], ["compare", "GT"]]`
  with positions `%{0 => {1, 1}, 1 => {1, 9}, 2 => {1, 7}}`.
- **`Types.position_table :: %{non_neg_integer() => position()}`** and
  **`Types.position :: {line :: pos_integer(), column :: pos_integer()}`**
  (types.ex lines 157 and 149). The table is keyed by 0-based instruction
  index.
- **`Types.instruction_list :: [instruction()]`**,
  **`Types.instruction :: [binary() | value()]`** (types.ex lines 143 and 136)
  - plain lists of lists, no structs, JSON-shaped.
- **There is no `Predicator.Compiled` module and no `compile_with_spans/1` in
  3.8.0.** `deps/predicator/lib/predicator/compiled.ex` does not exist, and the
  only `compile_with_*` definition in the whole dependency is
  `compile_with_positions/1`.
- **Error shape on compile failure is a bare string.** Both `compile/1` and
  `compile_with_positions/1` format the parse error into
  `"#{message} at line #{line}, column #{column}"` and return
  `{:error, binary()}`. There is no struct and no `:position` field on that
  return value.
- **Structured positions are reachable one level down.**
  `Predicator.parse/1` returns
  `{:ok, ast} | {:error, binary(), pos_integer(), pos_integer()}`
  (predicator.ex, `@spec` at line 359), so the compiler can get
  `{message, line, column}` as data rather than scraping the formatted string.
  `Predicator.Errors.ParseError` is a struct with
  `[:message, :line, :column, :position]`, all enforced, where `position` is
  `{line, column}` (errors/parse_error.ex lines 27-34) - but nothing in
  `Predicator.compile*/1` returns it.
- **`Predicator.Errors.put_position/2`** attaches a position to any error
  struct that has the field, and is a no-op otherwise (errors.ex lines 31-38).
- **Evaluation seam for st-af3.** `evaluate/3` accepts either a binary
  expression or a pre-compiled instruction list, plus a `:positions` option
  carrying the side table (predicator.ex lines 96, 144-179). The runtime error
  structs (`EvaluationError`, `TypeMismatchError`, `UndefinedVariableError`)
  carry `:position`. `Predicator.Context.new/2` and `evaluate/3` accept
  `on_unbound: :error` (predicator.ex lines 55-68), the policy knob ADR-0014
  item 5 commits to.

#### What 4.0.0 changes (published 2026-08-09, not yet pinned here)

Verified against a local checkout of predicator-ex at 4.0.0 by the research
sub-agent; this repo's `deps/` still holds 3.8.0, so these are not readable
from this worktree.

- **`Predicator.Compiled` exists**, with
  `defstruct instructions: [], positions: %{}, segment_positions: %{}` where
  `positions` holds either a position table or a span table depending on which
  function built it, plus `new/3`. Its moduledoc is emphatic that it is an
  in-memory value and not a wire format: persist the instructions or, better,
  the source, because nothing validates that a table came from the instructions
  it is attached to. ADR-0014 item 2 already records why that hazard does not
  apply here - conds are compiled in-process and no instruction list is stored.
- **`compile_with_positions/1` becomes `{:ok, %Compiled{}}`**, no longer a
  three-tuple, and **`compile_with_spans/1`** joins it. `compile/1` and
  `compile!/1` are unchanged and still return a bare instruction list.
- **`evaluate/3` accepts a `%Compiled{}` directly** and threads the table
  itself; passing both the struct and an explicit `:positions` is rejected.
- **`@type span :: {start :: position(), end_exclusive :: position()}`** with
  exclusive ends matching LSP ranges, plus `span_table`. `:span` is added to
  `EvaluationError`, `TypeMismatchError` and `UndefinedVariableError`;
  `ParseError` gets no `:span` and **loses** `:line`/`:column`, keeping only
  `:message` and `:position`.
- **`=` is no longer equality** - `==`/`===` only, and a bare `=` in expression
  position is a parse error. This is what makes st-2pj a corpus-converter sweep
  and not just a version bump.
- `Evaluator.unbound_loads_with_locations/1`, `Predicator.Instructions`
  (`required_isa/1` and friends), a statement layer (`execute/1,2,3`,
  `compile_program*`), and `segment_positions` for per-assignment-segment
  blame are new. The ISA stays at version 3. Minimum Elixir rises to 1.18 and
  `jason` is dropped, leaving predicator with no runtime dependencies.

### 10. The v1 reference at `../statifier`

Recorded as a comparison point, not a template.

- **No separate compiler.** v1 fused preparation into validation:
  `Statifier.Validator.finalize/2` (validator.ex lines 65-101) runs
  `ExpressionCompiler.compile_document/1`, then `Document.build_lookup_maps/1`,
  then `build_hierarchy_cache/1`, then `mark_as_validated/1`, mutating **the
  same `%Document{}`** rather than producing a distinct type. Invalid documents
  skip optimization, so one struct type has two shapes.
- **No interning.** State ids stayed strings everywhere. `%Document{}` carried
  `state_lookup` (id to state), `transitions_by_source`, a `hierarchy_cache`,
  a `validated` flag, and kept the nested `states` tree live alongside the flat
  map. `%State{}` carried `parent` as a **string id**, plus `depth` and
  `document_order`; `%Transition{}` carried `source` as a string id and its own
  `document_order`.
- **Hierarchy via a 376-line cache.** `Statifier.HierarchyCache` materialized
  `ancestor_paths`, an O(n^2) `lcca_matrix` over all state pairs,
  `descendant_sets` as MapSets of ids, `parallel_ancestors`, and
  `parallel_regions`, plus self-reported `build_time`/`memory_usage` and a
  `validate_cache/2` consistency checker. Every `StateHierarchy` function was
  cache-first with an uncached walking fallback, so correctness never depended
  on the prepare pass having run. `depth` was written and never read.
- **Document order came from the parser**, as a running count of all elements
  seen (`LocationTracker.document_order/1`), and sorting was
  `Enum.sort_by(& &1.document_order)`. Exit sets were computed at runtime by
  filtering the active leaf set through a four-predicate disjunction rather
  than the Appendix D formulation.
- **Expressions**: predicator 3.5.0, precompiled at prepare time into
  `compiled_cond` / `compiled_*_expr` slots **alongside** the raw string and
  the static attribute - the `x` / `x_expr` / `compiled_x_expr` triples
  [`docs/architecture.md:80-82`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/docs/architecture.md#L80-L82) names. Compile failure was a *warning*, leaving
  `compiled_* = nil` and letting the runtime recompile the string on the fly.
  `SendAction` resolved static, then compiled, then raw-compiled-at-runtime,
  then a hardcoded default. `location` was never precompiled;
  `Predicator.context_location/2` ran at every assignment. `Param`, `LogAction`,
  `RaiseAction`, `InvokeAction` and `Data` had no compiled fields at all.
- **Locations survived** into the compiled form (per-node `source_location`
  plus per-attribute location fields), but were recovered by regex-scanning the
  raw XML for the nth occurrence of a tag name, so they were approximate.
- **Per-node identity**: only `document_order`, and only on Document, State,
  Transition and Data. Executable content got no index and no id - actions were
  identified by position in their list. This is exactly the gap ADR-0012 item 3
  closes with `c_index`.

## Architecture Documentation

The pipeline is `XML -> Parser (Saxy SAX to generic DOM) -> Lowering -> Document
-> Validator -> Compiler -> Machine -> Interpreter -> {state, [effect]}`
([`docs/architecture.md:40-58`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/docs/architecture.md#L40-L58)). This bead is the Compiler arrow, and it is the
boundary where the "make invalid states unrepresentable" principle
([`docs/architecture.md:33-36`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/docs/architecture.md#L33-L36)) is actually cashed in: the interpreter accepts
only a Machine.

Settled decisions this pass implements rather than re-argues:

- ADR-0005 - full configuration, interned integer indexes, flat document-order
  array, parent pointers, descendant ranges, both index directions.
- ADR-0012 item 3 (detailed in `docs/observability.md` Constraint 3) -
  locations retained on compiled states, transitions and executable content;
  compiler-assigned document-order `t_index`/`c_index`; locations and indexes
  compile-time-immutable.
- ADR-0014 - the expression sum type, the table travelling with the
  instructions, spans always on, and what an expression failure names.
- ADR-0004 - predicator is the datamodel, permanently.
- ADR-0002/ADR-0003 - the Machine must be plain data with no pids or adapters,
  and the Appendix D predicates (`is_compound_state?` and friends) live on the
  Machine, not on the Document
  ([`lib/statifier/document/state.ex:76-82`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document/state.ex#L76-L82)).

Local conventions the pass inherits: `{reason, message, location}` errors
collected into a sorted list rather than raised; a closed tagged-tuple reason
union declared once with a constructor per reason; `@spec` on public
functions; state/session-first argument order; sabotage lines on every test
asserting `lib/` behavior.

## Open Questions

These are recorded rather than asked; no human was available during this
research.

1. **Does st-wju.1 bump the predicator pin, or take ADR-0014's stopgap?**
   [`docs/architecture.md:78`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/docs/architecture.md#L78), [`docs/datamodel.md:36`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/docs/datamodel.md#L36),
   [`docs/observability.md:82`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/docs/observability.md#L82), [`docs/adr/0014-expression-spans-in-cond-diagnostics.md:71`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/docs/adr/0014-expression-spans-in-cond-diagnostics.md#L71)
   and [`lib/statifier/document.ex:18`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document.ex#L18) all state the compiled arm as
   `{:compiled, %Predicator.Compiled{}, source}`, which needs predicator 4.0.
   4.0.0 published on 2026-08-09 (the date of this research), so the shape is
   now reachable - but this repo is pinned `~> 3.8`, and the bump is bead
   st-2pj, open at P3, whose acceptance criteria include sweeping the corpus
   converters from `=` to `==` and a full green including the conformance
   suites. The candidates:
   (a) add a dependency on st-2pj, bump the pin, and build the documented shape
   directly - the outcome ADR-0014 item 1 prefers ("target spans directly and
   skip the intermediate point shape"), at the cost of pulling a P3 chore with
   a corpus sweep into a P1 path;
   (b) take item 1's stopgap at 3.8 and store the point table in the same slot,
   which means the Phase 1 value is not literally `%Predicator.Compiled{}` and
   so does not match what four documents and one moduledoc currently say - and
   the `mix adr.judge` ADR-0014 rule ("compiles without spans", "drops the span
   table from the compiled-expression value") judges `lib/statifier/` diffs at
   `--profile merge`, so the stopgap needs an explicit ADR-0014-item-1 citation
   in the code to survive it;
   (c) split the bead so the interning half lands at 3.8 and the expression
   half follows the bump.
   This is the plan-time decision. Note also that if (a) is taken, the
   `%Predicator.Compiled{}` value carries `positions` **and**
   `segment_positions`, and 4.0's `ParseError` no longer has `:line`/`:column`,
   only `:position`.
2. **What a cond compile failure names, and in whose coordinate space.** The
   bead requires the error to name "the expression source, its location, and
   predicator's position". At 3.8, `compile_with_positions/1` returns only a
   formatted string on failure, so getting `{line, column}` as data means
   calling `Predicator.parse/1` first (which returns
   `{:error, message, line, column}`), or re-parsing on the error path, or
   scraping the message. At 4.0 the same problem exists in a different shape:
   the compile functions still do not return a `%ParseError{}`. Independently
   unresolved either way: whether the compiler translates predicator's
   expression-relative position into a document `Location` via
   `attribute_locations[:cond].start_offset` at compile time, or stores it raw
   and leaves the arithmetic to the diagnostic. ADR-0014's consequences quote a
   message in document terms ("columns 22-27 of ..."), which suggests but does
   not mandate translating at the point of reporting.
3. **Whether `Content.text` folds into the `expr()` sum type.**
   [`docs/architecture.md:80-82`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/docs/architecture.md#L80-L82) says every foo/fooexpr pair stores one `expr`
   value, and `Content` is the only Phase 1 node with a static/expression
   alternative (`text` vs `expr`). Whether the compiler stores
   `{:static, text}` for the text case or keeps two fields is not settled
   anywhere.
4. **What `donedata` compiles to.** ADR-0012 item 3 names states, transitions
   and executable content; `Donedata` and its `Content` child are neither a
   transition nor a `content_node()`. Whether donedata content gets a `c_index`
   of its own, or is reached only through its owning final state, is
   unspecified.
5. **Whether the `<initial>` element's transition gets a `t_index`.** The
   bead's design says `t_index` is "document-order identity across the
   machine". `<initial>` transitions and history default transitions are
   transitions in the Document but are not selectable by
   `select_transitions`. Giving them indexes keeps the numbering dense over the
   source; excluding them keeps the index space equal to the selectable set.
   Not settled.
6. **The nameless-state case.** `id` is optional on every kind
   ([`lib/statifier/document/state.ex:51`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/lib/statifier/document/state.ex#L51)), and the validator excludes `nil` and
   `""` ids from its uniqueness set. Every such state still needs an index, so
   `id_to_index` is necessarily partial while `index_to_id` is total-with-nils.
   The bead's acceptance criterion says "id-to-index and index-to-id both
   directions" without saying which side is partial.
7. **[`docs/datamodel.md:3-4`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/4254e8182ab9f1fa98f022ac57b9d4388e42b828/docs/datamodel.md#L3-L4) and ADR-0004 line 16 still say `~> 3.5`.** The
   real pin has been `~> 3.8` since st-lqg. Noted as observed drift; no action
   taken here.
