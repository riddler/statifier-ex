# Research: `<datamodel>`/`<data>` parsing and early/late binding

**Date**: 2026-08-12T21:21:31-0600
**Git Commit**: 13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838
**Branch**: st-af3.3-datamodel-data-binding
**Bead**: st-af3.3

## Research Question

st-af3.3 asks for `<datamodel>`/`<data>` lowering, validation, compilation, and
early/late binding. This document maps what exists today at every layer that
work would touch: DOM lowering, the Document structs, the validator checks,
`Statifier.Compiler.Expressions`, `MachineState.datamodel` and `new/2`'s
`:datamodel` option, `SystemVariables.initial/2` seeding, the commented seam in
`Statifier.Interpreter.initialize/2`, how the conformance corpus expresses
`<datamodel>`/`<data>`, and what harness support exists. It also records what
the spec text actually says, read from the local cache rather than from memory.

## Summary

**Nothing about `<datamodel>` or `<data>` exists in `lib/` today beyond two
root attributes.** `<scxml datamodel="...">` and `<scxml binding="early|late">`
are lowered onto `%Statifier.Document{}` and copied unchanged onto
`%Statifier.Machine{}`, and nothing reads either one. The `<datamodel>` and
`<data>` *elements* are not in the lowering dispatch map, so any document
containing them fails to lower with `{:unsupported_element, "datamodel"}` and
`{:unsupported_element, "data"}` before validation ever runs.

The runtime datamodel map is real but is seeded only from two sources:
`MachineState.new/2`'s `:datamodel` option (an author-supplied map, default
`%{}`) merged **under** `SystemVariables.initial/2`'s four spec-5.10 system
variables. Only two functions in the whole of `lib/` write
`MachineState.datamodel`: `new/2` and `put_event/2`. Every consumer already
exists and works - `Statifier.Evaluator.context/1` builds a real
`Predicator.Context` with `on_unbound: :error`, `Selection.condition_match/2`
evaluates real conds and raises `error.execution` on failure, and
`Interpreter.Content` does the same for content nodes. What is missing is the
producer.

The two skipped hooks are both named in comments at exactly the pseudocode
positions they belong at: `interpret`'s early-binding preamble
([`lib/statifier/interpreter.ex:163-165`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/interpreter.ex#L163-L165)) and `enterStates`' per-state
`binding == "late" and s.isFirstEntry` step
([`lib/statifier/interpreter/exit_entry.ex:619-623`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/interpreter/exit_entry.ex#L619-L623)).

Four factual corrections to the bead's framing came out of this research, all
detailed below: the blocked-corpus count is **105, not 107**; spec 5.3.2's
failure clause says "create an empty data element ... with the specified id"
and names `src`/children/environment rather than `expr`, with "undefined"
coming from the ECMAScript-specific Appendix B.2.1; the spec makes `src` fetch
time **binding-controlled**, not load-time-only, which is at odds with
[`docs/datamodel.md:22-23`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/docs/datamodel.md#L22-L23); and test245's `Var2` *is* declared by a `<data>`
element in the generated corpus file, contrary to the bead note.

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Lowering: `<datamodel>` and `<data>` are hard errors today

The dispatch map has thirteen entries and neither name is among them
([`lib/statifier/lowering.ex:53-67`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/lowering.ex#L53-L67)): `scxml`, `state`, `parallel`, `final`,
`history`, `initial`, `transition`, `onentry`, `onexit`, `raise`, `log`,
`donedata`, `content`. An element that misses the map becomes
`Error.unsupported(name, location)` ([`lib/statifier/lowering.ex:144`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/lowering.ex#L144)), and
`finalize/2` ([`lib/statifier/lowering.ex:151-155`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/lowering.ex#L151-L155)) returns `{:error, errors}`
for any non-empty error list - never a partial document. So today a document
with a `<datamodel>` fails to lower entirely.

The root attributes are a different mechanism and already work.
`build_scxml/2` reads them at [`lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex:52`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex#L52) and
`:61-62`, with `@binding_values = %{"early" => :early, "late" => :late}` at
[`lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex:31`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex#L31). `Attributes.atom/4`
([`lib/statifier/lowering/attributes.ex:61-73`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/lowering/attributes.ex#L61-L73)) maps an out-of-range value onto
the default rather than erroring, which is what makes the validator's
read-the-raw-source approach necessary (section 3).

**The builder pattern a `build_datamodel/2` + `build_data/2` pair would
follow.** Every builder lowers its children first via
`Lowering.walk_children/2`, builds its own struct, then places tagged children
through the container's own `place/3` clause; list slots are prepended and
reversed once by `reverse_lists/1`
([`lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex:376-481`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex#L376-L481)). `build_log/2`
([`lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex:242-260`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex#L242-L260)) is the closest shape for
`<data expr=...>`: raw uncompiled `expr` string plus an `attribute_locations`
entry. `build_content/2` ([`lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex:303-321`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex#L303-L321)) is the
closest shape for `<data>child content</data>`: it is the one element exempt
from the stray-text rule, reading `element.children` directly rather than
calling `walk_children/2` (which would flag its text as a stray-text error),
and it sets `text` to `DOM.text/1`'s **verbatim, untrimmed** concatenation.
`build_raise/2` ([`lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex:212-232`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/lowering/builders.ex#L212-L232)) is the pattern
for a required attribute: a missing `event` yields
`{nil, [Error.missing_attribute(...)]}` because `:event` is `@enforce_keys`'d.
`<data>`'s `id` is required by spec 5.3.1 and would follow that shape.

Relevant error reasons already exist
([`lib/statifier/lowering/error.ex:21-27`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/lowering/error.ex#L21-L27)): `:unsupported_element`,
`:misplaced_element`, `:stray_text`, `:unexpected_root`, `:missing_attribute`,
`:foreign_element`.

### 2. Document structs: no `Data`, no per-state slot

`%Statifier.Document{}` ([`lib/statifier/document.ex:110-135`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/document.ex#L110-L135)) carries
`datamodel: String.t() | nil` and `binding: :early | :late` and has no slot for
`<data>` entries. `%Statifier.Document.State{}`
([`lib/statifier/document/state.ex:93-106`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/document/state.ex#L93-L106)) has `kind`, `location`, `id`,
`initial`, `initial_element`, `states`, `transitions`, `onentry`, `onexit`,
`history_type`, `donedata`, `attribute_locations` - **no `datamodel` field**,
which late binding needs since spec 5.3.3 binds a state's own `<data>` on its
first entry.

Two contracts constrain any new `Statifier.Document.Data` struct:

- **Nothing under `lib/statifier/document/` may reference `Predicator`**
  ([`lib/statifier/document.ex:14-20`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/document.ex#L14-L20)) - `expr` and child content stay raw
  strings, compiled only at Machine-build time.
- **`attribute_locations` holds value spans only, and an entry exists only for
  an attribute actually written** ([`lib/statifier/document.ex:22-39`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/document.ex#L22-L39)). That is
  the "was it written or defaulted" question a diagnostic needs, and it is what
  the `Enums` validator check reads back through `Location.slice/2`.

The `Content` struct is the explicit precedent for representing an illegal
shape rather than refusing to build it
([`lib/statifier/validator/checks/content.ex:6-10`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/validator/checks/content.ex#L6-L10)): `expr` and `text` are both
nilable and both settable at once "precisely so this check can report the shape
rather than lowering refusing to build it." `<data>`'s three-way
`expr`/`src`/children exclusion is the same division of labor.

### 3. Validator: twelve checks, none touching `datamodel`

`Statifier.Validator.validate/2` ([`lib/statifier/validator.ex:75-87`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/validator.ex#L75-L87)) runs a
fixed `@checks` list ([`lib/statifier/validator.ex:47-60`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/validator.ex#L47-L60)) - `Ids`, `Targets`,
`InitialTargets`, `InitialElement`, `History`, `Final`, `FinalParent`,
`DefaultEntry`, `Donedata`, `Content`, `Boilerplate`, `Enums` - flat-maps every
check's error list (never fail-fast) and sorts by `location.start_offset`.
Adding a check means writing `Checks.Foo.check/2` and appending its capture;
there is no behaviour and no dynamic discovery.

Three findings bear directly on this bead:

- **`Ids` does not see anything but states.** `check/2`
  ([`lib/statifier/validator/checks/ids.ex:33-37`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/validator/checks/ids.ex#L33-L37)) ignores the `Context`
  entirely and uses its own `flatten/1`
  ([`lib/statifier/validator/checks/ids.ex:52-54`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/validator/checks/ids.ex#L52-L54)) over `state.states`.
  `Context`'s own `walk/3` ([`lib/statifier/validator/context.ex:85-105`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/validator/context.ex#L85-L105))
  likewise recurses only into `states`. A `Data` struct with its own `id` is
  invisible to both. Its uniqueness scope is a separate decision (spec 3.14
  types `id` as an XML Schema ID, which is document-global) and a separate
  threading job.
- **The `datamodel` attribute is unchecked anywhere.** `Enums.check/2`
  ([`lib/statifier/validator/checks/enums.ex:52-55`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/validator/checks/enums.ex#L52-L55)) computes only
  `transition_type_errors/1` and `binding_errors/2`. `binding` already has its
  allow-list `@bindings = ["early", "late"]`
  ([`lib/statifier/validator/checks/enums.ex:41`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/validator/checks/enums.ex#L41)) and its error constructor
  `Error.scxml_bad_binding/2`. There is no `:scxml_bad_datamodel` reason in the
  closed union ([`lib/statifier/validator/error.ex:33-56`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/validator/error.ex#L33-L56)) and no constructor
  for one. `binding_errors/2`
  ([`lib/statifier/validator/checks/enums.ex:66-70`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/validator/checks/enums.ex#L66-L70)) is the exact template for
  a `datamodel` allow-list of `["predicator", "elixir"]`
  ([`docs/datamodel.md:6-7`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/docs/datamodel.md#L6-L7)).
- **The mutual-exclusion pattern already exists twice.**
  `Checks.Content.check_content/1`
  ([`lib/statifier/validator/checks/content.ex:56-64`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/validator/checks/content.ex#L56-L64)) reports `expr` plus
  non-blank text, treating whitespace-only text as absent via `blank?/1`
  ([`lib/statifier/validator/checks/content.ex:66-67`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/validator/checks/content.ex#L66-L67)) because pretty-printed
  XML leaves whitespace text nodes that are formatting, not payload. The error
  is located at the containing element's own span, not either half's.
  `Checks.Donedata.offending?/1`
  ([`lib/statifier/validator/checks/donedata.ex:32-34`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/validator/checks/donedata.ex#L32-L34)) is the sibling shape for
  a placement rule.

The shared `out_of_range/5` helper
([`lib/statifier/validator/checks/enums.ex:72-81`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/validator/checks/enums.ex#L72-L81)) is the mechanism that reads
raw source back through `Location.slice/2` - necessary because
`Attributes.atom/4` has already collapsed an invalid value onto the default.

### 4. Compiler: where `<data>` expressions would compile

`Statifier.Compiler.compile/1` ([`lib/statifier/compiler.ex:120-190`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/compiler.ex#L120-L190)) runs one
depth-first numbering walk that assigns state `index`/`parent`/`last`, resolves
`initial` post-order, and hands out `t_index`/`c_index` inline, then three
deferred build passes: transitions ([`lib/statifier/compiler.ex:465-516`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/compiler.ex#L465-L516)),
executable content ([`lib/statifier/compiler.ex:541-582`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/compiler.ex#L541-L582)), and donedata
([`lib/statifier/compiler.ex:599-635`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/compiler.ex#L599-L635)). Each pass's errors flow through the
shared `collect/1` reducer ([`lib/statifier/compiler.ex:671-684`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/compiler.ex#L671-L684)) and are
re-sorted together by `location.start_offset`
([`lib/statifier/compiler.ex:155-161`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/compiler.ex#L155-L161)).

`Expressions.compile/3` has exactly three call sites today, and each supplies
an `owner_ref` and a location that is the attribute's own value span when
written, the owning node's `location` otherwise:

| call site | owner | location helper |
|---|---|---|
| `build_cond/2` ([`lib/statifier/compiler.ex:522-524`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/compiler.ex#L522-L524)) | `{:transition, t_index}` | `cond_location/1` (`:531-536`) |
| `build_content_node/2` for `<log expr>` ([`lib/statifier/compiler.ex:567-568`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/compiler.ex#L567-L568)) | `{:content, c_index}` | `expr_location/1` (`:588-591`) |
| `build_content_expr/2` for `<donedata><content expr>` ([`lib/statifier/compiler.ex:646-647`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/compiler.ex#L646-L647)) | `{:donedata, index}` | `content_expr_location/1` (`:655-664`) |

The `owner_ref` union ([`lib/statifier/compiler/expressions.ex:31-34`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/compiler/expressions.ex#L31-L34)) has three
arms and would need a `:data` arm, as the bead anticipates. Note the
`{:donedata, index}` precedent: it is the **owning final state's index**, not a
dense counter, because `<donedata>` content is deliberately excluded from
`c_index` space ([`lib/statifier/machine/content.ex:20-23`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/machine/content.ex#L20-L23)). A per-state
`<data>` collection has the same choice available.

`Expressions.compile/3` always calls `Predicator.compile_with_spans/1`
([`lib/statifier/compiler/expressions.ex:65`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/compiler/expressions.ex#L65)) per ADR-0014 item 1, and stores
the `%Predicator.Compiled{}` whole. `Expressions.static/1`
([`lib/statifier/compiler/expressions.ex:80`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/compiler/expressions.ex#L80)) is the `{:static, v}` arm for a
literal with no expression to evaluate - the shape `<data>child content</data>`
most resembles.

`%Statifier.Machine{}` ([`lib/statifier/machine.ex:63-73`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/machine.ex#L63-L73)) has `states`,
`id_to_index`, `transitions`, `contents`, `name`, `datamodel`, `binding`,
`location`. `datamodel` and `binding` are copied straight from the Document at
[`lib/statifier/compiler.ex:180-181`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/compiler.ex#L180-L181) and are the *only* datamodel-related data
that survives compilation. `%Statifier.Machine.State{}`
([`lib/statifier/machine/state.ex:46-83`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/machine/state.ex#L46-L83)) has no data slot either. Two
structural precedents exist for a new collection: a dense index-keyed tuple
(`transitions`, `contents`) for a document-wide flat collection, or the
`donedata_acc` shape ([`lib/statifier/compiler.ex:94-102`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/compiler.ex#L94-L102), `:593-598`) - a map
keyed by owning state index, folded onto the state struct post-walk
(`with_donedata/2`, [`lib/statifier/compiler.ex:192-199`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/compiler.ex#L192-L199)).

### 5. The runtime datamodel and its two writers

`MachineState.new/2` ([`lib/statifier/machine_state.ex:210-227`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/machine_state.ex#L210-L227)):

```elixir
datamodel: Map.merge(author_datamodel, SystemVariables.initial(machine, session_id)),
```

`author_datamodel` is `Keyword.get(opts, :datamodel, %{})`. The system
variables are merged **over** it, so an author map can never shadow a system
variable ([`lib/statifier/machine_state.ex:97-101`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/machine_state.ex#L97-L101)). `datamodel` is typed
`map()` deliberately so that filling in real evaluation "only adds content, not
shape" ([`lib/statifier/machine_state.ex:156-163`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/machine_state.ex#L156-L163)).

A repo-wide grep confirms exactly two writers of the field:
[`lib/statifier/machine_state.ex:220`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/machine_state.ex#L220) (`new/2`) and
[`lib/statifier/machine_state.ex:289`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/machine_state.ex#L289) (`put_event/2`). Every other `datamodel:`
hit in `lib/` is the unrelated `Document`/`Machine` attribute string.

**`SystemVariables.initial/2` is the seeding precedent this bead should copy.**
It binds `"_event" => nil` ([`lib/statifier/evaluator/system_variables.ex:55`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/lib/statifier/evaluator/system_variables.ex#L55))
rather than omitting the key, and its moduledoc argues the case at length

### 10. The boundness seeding requirement (st-unt)

`docs/research/260812-st-unt-boundness-sentinel-vs-on-unbound-error.md`
probed predicator 5.0.0 empirically with a context shaped like `context/1`'s:

| datamodel map | expression | result |
|---|---|---|
| `Var1` absent | `Var1 === undefined` | `{:error, UndefinedVariableError}` |
| `Var1` absent | `Var1 !== undefined` | `{:error, UndefinedVariableError}` |
| `%{"Var1" => :undefined}` | `Var1 === undefined` | `{:ok, true}` |
| `%{"Var1" => :undefined}` | `Var1 !== undefined` | `{:ok, false}` |
| `%{"Var1" => nil}` | `Var1 === undefined` | `{:ok, true}` |
| `%{"Var1" => 1}` | `Var1 === undefined` | `{:ok, false}` |
| `%{"Var1" => 1}` | `Var1 !== undefined` | `{:ok, true}` |

to `nil` or `:undefined`), exactly as `_event` is seeded, not absent. That
conclusion needs no ADR amendment and no further corpus change.

### 11. What the spec actually says

Read from the local cache (`spec-cache/scxml-rec.html`,
`spec-cache/appendix-d.txt`), not from memory. Three quotes matter most.

**5.3.2 `<data>` children**, verbatim:

> In a conformant SCXML document, a `<data>` element MAY have either a 'src' or
> an 'expr' attribute, but MUST NOT have both. Furthermore, if either attribute
> is present, the element MUST NOT have any children. Thus 'src', 'expr' and
> children are mutually exclusive in the `<data>` element.

and, in the same clause:

> If the 'expr' attribute is present, the Platform MUST evaluate the
> corresponding expression at the time specified by the 'binding' attribute of
> `<scxml>` [...] If the 'src' attribute is present, the Platform MUST fetch
> the specified object at the time specified by the 'binding' attribute of
> `<scxml>` [...] If the value specified for a `<data>` element (by 'src',
> children, or the environment) is not a legal data value, the SCXML Processor
> MUST raise place error.execution in the internal event queue and MUST create
> an empty data element in the data model with the specified id.

("MUST raise place" is a verbatim typo in the REC.) Two things follow. The
failure clause says **"create an empty data element ... with the specified
id"**, not "bind to undefined"; the undefined language is ECMAScript-specific,
in B.2.1: "If no value is assigned, the SCXML Processor MUST assign the
variable the default value ECMAScript undefined." And the clause names
`src`/children/environment, **not `expr`** - an `expr` failure reaches
`error.execution` through 5.9.3's general rule instead: "If a value expression
does not return a legal data value, the SCXML Processor MUST place the error
'error.execution' in the internal event queue." Both routes land in the same
place for this codebase's purposes, but the bead's "per spec 5.3" citation is
imprecise about which clause carries which half.

**5.3.3 Data Binding**, verbatim:

> When 'binding' is assigned the value "early" (the default), the SCXML
> Processor MUST create all data elements and assign their initial values at
> document initialization time. When 'binding' is assigned the value "late",
> the SCXML Processor MUST create the data elements at document initialization
> time, but MUST assign the specified initial value to a given data element
> only when the state that contains it is entered for the first time, before
> any `<onentry>` markup.

Note "**create** the data elements at document initialization time" holds under
*both* bindings - which is independently the same conclusion st-unt reached
empirically for predicator. Late binding defers the *value*, not the
declaration. 5.9 adds that under late binding, "accessing data substructure in
expressions before the corresponding `<data>` element is loaded MUST yield the
same execution-time behavior as accessing non-existent data substructure in a
loaded `<data>` instance."

    initializeDatamodel(datamodel, doc)

executeGlobalScriptElement(doc)

```

```

`initializeDatamodel` / `initializeDataModel` and `executeGlobalScriptElement`
have **no procedure body anywhere in `appendix-d.txt`** - only the three call
sites. The casing inconsistency between the two call sites is verbatim in the
spec. So ADR-0002's "port the pseudocode literally" has nothing to port here
beyond the two call sites and the `isFirstEntry` flag; the body's content comes
from clause 5.3, not from Appendix D.

Also relevant if `<invoke>` ever lands (6.4): a `<param>` whose name matches a
top-level `<data>` id overrides that data element's value, and "any value
specified in the `<data>` element is ignored". That is the same override the
5.3.2 "values provided by the environment at instantiation time" sentence
describes, and it is the spec's own name for what `MachineState.new/2`'s
`:datamodel` option does.

## Architecture Documentation

- **ADR-0004** - predicator is the datamodel. `datamodel="predicator"` is the
  accepted value, with `elixir` as a v1-continuity alias
  ([`docs/datamodel.md:6-7`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/docs/datamodel.md#L6-L7)). Neither is validated today.
- **ADR-0002** - literal Appendix D port. Both skipped hooks are already
  commented at their pseudocode positions, and both name the reason. Since
  `initializeDataModel` has no pseudocode body, the port obligation here is the
  call sites and the `isFirstEntry` flag; the body comes from clause 5.3.
- **ADR-0003** - pure core. Fetching a `src` URI is I/O and cannot happen
  inside the core; see the open question below about *when* the spec permits
  the fetch.
- **ADR-0005** - interned indexes below the boundary. A `<data>` id is an author
  string that must live in the datamodel map as a string key (predicator reads
  string keys), unlike state ids, which intern to integers.
- **ADR-0012 / ADR-0014** - retained locations and expression spans. A `<data>`
  expr compiles through `compile_with_spans/1` like every other expression, and
  a failure names the owning node's constraint-3 identity, the source string,
  the predicator error struct, and its span. `on_unbound: :error` (item 5) is
  what makes seeding versus absence observably different.
- **ADR-0019** - the macrostep round budget. Not directly touched, but early
  binding runs before the initialization macrostep's fold, so an
  `error.execution` raised during early binding enters the same internal queue
  the first fold drains.

## Open Questions

These are recorded for the planning stage; this research answers none of them,
and no human was available to settle any during this pass.

1. **`src` fetch timing contradicts the standing design doc.**
   [`docs/datamodel.md:22-23`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/docs/datamodel.md#L22-L23) and the bead both say `src` is "fetched at
   document-load time only ... a compile-time concern, never a runtime fetch".
   Spec 5.3.2 says the Platform "MUST fetch the specified object **at the time
   specified by the 'binding' attribute of `<scxml>`**", which under
   `binding="late"` is the first entry of the containing state - a runtime
   fetch. `grep -i fetch` over the whole REC returns four hits and none of them
   qualifies this. Exactly one corpus file uses `src`
   (`mandatory/data/test552_test.exs`, root-level, default `binding="early"`),
   so the two readings are indistinguishable on today's corpus. Whether to
   follow the doc or the spec, and whether `docs/datamodel.md` needs amending,
   is a design call. ADR-0003 (pure core) makes a runtime fetch expensive: it
   would have to become an effect.

2. **The uniqueness scope of a `<data>` id.** Spec 3.14 types `id` as an XML
   Schema ID, which is document-global and shared with state ids. The bead says
   "unique against the existing ids check", which reads as one shared namespace,
   but `Checks.Ids` today walks only `state.states` and reports at a state's own
   location. Whether `<data>` ids join that check's flatten or get their own
   check module (and whether a `<data id="s1">` colliding with `<state id="s1">`
   is an error) is unsettled.

3. **Where `<data>` entries live on the Machine.** Two precedents exist: a
   dense index-keyed tuple like `transitions`/`contents`, or a per-owning-state
   map folded onto the state struct like `donedata`. Late binding needs
   per-state grouping; early binding needs a flat document-order sequence. The
   `{:donedata, index}` `owner_ref` arm shows a non-dense owner key is
   acceptable.

4. **Precedence between `MachineState.new/2`'s `:datamodel` option and
   document-declared `<data>`.** The bead defers this to the plan. Spec 5.3.2
   is explicit that the environment wins - "The SCXML Processor MUST use any
   values provided by the environment at instantiation time in place of those
   contained in the top-level `<data>` elements" - and 6.4 says the same for an
   `<invoke>` `<param>`/`namelist`. Note that today `new/2` merges the option
   map *under* the system variables, and the option is applied at `new/2` time
   while `<data>` binding would happen later in `initialize/2`, so a naive
   ordering would let `<data>` overwrite the environment - the opposite of what
   the spec requires. The option is also currently document-agnostic: it does
   not know which keys correspond to declared top-level `<data>` ids, which the
   spec's rule is scoped to.

5. **First-entry tracking for late binding.** Appendix D uses a mutable
   `s.isFirstEntry` flag. Nothing equivalent exists in `MachineState`, and
   `states_for_default_entry` is not a substitute (history restoration re-enters
   a state without default entry). Whether this becomes a `MapSet` on
   `MachineState`, a derived fact, or something else is a design call - and it
   interacts with the ADR-0012 resumability constraint, since it is interpreter
   position that must survive a `%MachineState{}` round trip.

6. **Whether a text body and an `expr` fold into one `expr()` value.** Inherited
   verbatim from st-wju.1's own open question about `Content.text` versus
   `Content.expr`. `<data>` has three sources (`expr`, children, `src`) that all
   produce one value, so the question is sharper here.

7. **What the `error.execution` payload for a failed `<data>` carries.**
   ADR-0014 item 4 commits to the fields but leaves the shape to
   implementation. `Cause.origin` needs a new arm; whether it is
   `{:data, index}` following `{:transition, t_index}`'s
   resolve-through-the-Machine shape is unsettled. Also unsettled: whether the
   id is bound to `nil` *before* the error is raised or after, and whether a
   compile-time-detectable bad expr (test277's `expr="return"`) fails at
   `Compiler.Expressions.compile/3` - making the whole document fail to
   compile - or is deferred to runtime. Spec 5.9.4 explicitly permits either
   ("MAY reject documents containing syntactically ill-formed expressions at
   document load time, or it MAY wait"), but test277 asserts the runtime
   behavior: it expects `error.execution` and then `Var1 === undefined`. A
   compile-time rejection would make that document unloadable and the test
   unpassable.

8. **Corpus payoff is not measurable within this bead.** Only 3 of the 105
   files are blocked by `{datamodel, data_elements}` alone; the rest also need
   features whose registry entries st-af3.8 owns flipping - including
   `conditional_transitions`, which is still `:unsupported` in the registry
   despite st-af3.2 having landed. Whether this bead flips
   `datamodel`/`data_elements` to `:supported` itself and ratchets those 3
   files, or leaves the whole registry change to st-af3.8, is a scoping call.
   The feature detector also has no `binding=` pattern, so nothing today
   detects that a document needs late binding.

9. **The `datamodel` attribute's accepted values, and what an absent one
   means.** [`docs/datamodel.md:6-7`](https://github.com/riddler/statifier-ex/blob/13d2fe68aa6304c1cf1dc2e91df5419aaee82838/docs/datamodel.md#L6-L7) names `predicator` with `elixir` as an
   alias. The corpus's XSL emits `datamodel="predicator"` uniformly. What a
   document with no `datamodel` attribute at all gets (the spec's default is
   the Platform's choice) is unstated here - most SCION corpus files omit it.
